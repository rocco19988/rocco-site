How I build a map

Making it look nice is the whole point

A map can play well and still feel empty. I go back over every area until it looks finished.

Layout

Block out the land, paths and main areas.

Gameplay

Make sure it's fun to run through and fight in.

Detail pass

Add props, plants and small touches so no spot looks bare.

Lighting

Set the time of day, colors and mood.

Playtest

Have friends play it and fix what feels off.
